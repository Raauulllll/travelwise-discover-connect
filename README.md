# TravelWise India

Build TravelWise, a modern Indian travel discovery and comparison platform (Flights, Trains, Buses) from scratch.

Include:
1. Brand & Navigation: Sticky header with 'TRAVELWISE' logo, nav links (Plan Trip, Explore, Compare, Travel Safe, Groups, Rewards, Price Trends, Eco Travel), language selector (EN/हिंदी), notifications, and profile dropdown (My Trips, Groups, KYC, Student Verification, Rewards).
2. Hero & Multimodal Search: Route selector (default Mumbai → Delhi), trip types (One Way, Round Trip, Multi-City), mode filter (All, Flights, Trains, Buses), flexible dates (±3 days fare chart), and traveller count.
3. Traveller Types & Preferences: Solo, Solo Woman, Group, Family, Accessibility Needs, Student. Preference filters (Cheapest, Fastest, Eco-friendly, Safest, Women-friendly, Accessible, Student, Best Overall).
4. Smart Recommendation & Journey Cards: Multi-factor scoring (price, duration, reliability, CO2, safety suitability, accessibility). Detailed journey cards for Flights, Trains (e.g., Vande Bharat, Rajdhani), Buses with badges (Best Overall, Greenest, Safest Match, Student Offer). Side-by-side comparison modal.
5. Price Intelligence: 'Travel when prices are low' monthly calendar for destinations (Goa, Manali, Jaipur, etc.), Destination Price Index with 'Travel now or later' gauge, price trends (7d/30d), and smart multimodal combos.
6. Solo Women Traveller Hub: Safety suitability scores (operator reliability, daytime arrival, transfer ratings), Women-Only Travel Groups, Verified Co-ed Groups, and Stay Solo planning.
7. Group Travel & Expense Splitter: Splitwise-style trip planner with member roster, custom/equal splits across categories, and settlement simplification algorithm with net balances and 'Mark as paid'.
8. Accessibility-First Travel: Step-free boarding, wheelchair assistance, accessible toilet filters, and journey accessibility score breakdowns.
9. Student Mode & Verification: Demo student ID verification flow and unlocked student discount fares.
10. Rewards, KYC & Sustainability: Eco-points, reward tiers (Explorer to Pro), demo KYC badge system, and CO2 emissions saved tracker. Responsive, mobile-first, and polished UI with comprehensive mock data.
